## Window shadow pops in / out around the zoom from / to an icon (0.7.7, device-unverified)
- User (recording 8227294a, a windowed app opened from its Home Screen icon): "the area around the window darkens like a sec after the window fully opens". Measured (specs/OPEN-DIM-0.7.7.md): a large drop shadow appears in ONE frame 0.45 s after the first dimming frame of each open (t = 2.093, 3.793, 5.577 s; mean luma -15 right of the window, -22 below, -8 above, decaying with distance), and disappears within two frames at the start of every close. The smooth darkening of the wallpaper during the open is the stock home screen blur / dim, no step.
- Cause (our port, not stock): the crop mask of `g2f_closecrop` (G2F.x, build 24816c1) is a layer mask over the item container bounds, which clips the shadow (a CALayer shadow outside the bounds) for as long as it exists: created in phase 1 of an open / close, released 0.56 - 0.58 s after phase 1 of an open (`[g2f] crop: mask released`, logs 291a0cf5 / 8025ca8b). Stock has no mask and fades the shadow in with the "style" animation from ~30 ms into the open (floor swap at phase 2; shadow code identical in 16.0 and 16.2).
- First fix (G2H.x, build ed3eb1b) inflated the crop mask by the shadow extent and REGRESSED the close (the crop only started after half of the mask's travel: wide card with the icon inside, user recordings 080e9505 / 7b244285). Replaced: the mask is exactly G2F's again (24816c1 crop timing); `g2h_maskshadow` now fades the shadow in over 0.22 s when the mask is dropped (instead of the pop) and leaves the start of a close alone (the shadow still disappears at once there; keeping it needs a wider mask = delayed crop). Diagnostics `g2h_dimdiag` (Backport162.debug): `[g2h] dim#N ...` per phase and every 50 ms for 1.5 s (log 27f5d0d0 shows shadowOpacity 1.0 / shadow layer r 70, opacity 0.35, offset y +35 from phase 1: the mask alone hides the shadow), `[g2h] mask#N set|sample` with the mask's logical / layer / presentation bounds at 0, 50, 100, 150, 250 ms, `[g2h] mask removed ...`. To check: the close crops with the overlay exactly as in 24816c1 (`pres` width of mask#N down to the icon box), the open's shadow fades in after the window has arrived. Unproven: the logs supplied with the recording are of another run (no `[g2f]` line, no home -> app transition), so the mask as the only cause is inferred from timing and shape.
