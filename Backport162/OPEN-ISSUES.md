15b. **Window corner resize mark disappears after ~2 s, corner drag then does nothing until the top strip is touched** (user video RPReplay_Final1791889960, Files window on the main display). NEEDS A DEBUG LOG to close: lines `[g2c] auto hide of the resize grabbers ...` and `[g2c] grabber presence N ... allowedTouchResizeCorners=0x..` show whether the corner mask is 0 while the mark is hidden (then `G1C_FS_ShouldEnableGrabbers` / occlusion is the cause, G1C.x) or non zero (then the system gesture type 0x22 / `_shouldLiveResizeGesture:receiveTouch:` in G1D.x refused the touch).
